# Booking Policy

## Booking Window

Customers can book flights up to 365 days in advance.

## Name Changes

Minor spelling corrections (up to 3 characters) are allowed free of charge.
Full passenger name changes are not permitted after ticket confirmation.

## Group Bookings

Bookings for 10 or more passengers qualify for group discounts and dedicated customer support.

## Booking Confirmation

An e-ticket and booking confirmation are sent via email and SMS immediately after successful payment.
